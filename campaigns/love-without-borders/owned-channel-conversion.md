# Discovery to Second Listen
## Implementation specification, not a live deployment

## Destination map

Accountability or relationship discussion: One More Chance visualizer, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SdK9K9vhaMo, followed by the official Sean Austin Reggae channel. This video ID is reused from the site's existing latest-release page; verify current playback before live integration.

Wedding, commitment or long-distance dedication: Fi Yu Forever, https://youtu.be/bXUj-hX-YWc, followed by another verified Sean Austin recording or the official channel.

Artist-profile and diaspora coverage: https://www.seanaustinmusic.com/ plus the relevant official video and https://www.youtube.com/@SeanAustinReggae.

Every outreach carries the website and channel in a compact block. Put the relevant listen first, with one appropriate public action. Ask editorial partners for official-video links or available embeds and artist credit. Give radio hosts an optional spoken website and channel signpost. Do not invent exclusive premieres or audience numbers.

## Website integration

The repository is `LBSconnect/SeanAustinMusic`. The materials in this directory are source drafts, not app routes. The standalone preview is outside the application's served assets and retains its noindex and preview notices.

For a future production change, inspect the current React application, reuse its design and navigation, verify video ownership and playback, register the real page route, set the correct canonical and SEO metadata, and link it from an appropriate existing page. Remove noindex and preview notices only from the actual production page after those checks. Do not email proposed slugs as live URLs or add them to a sitemap before deployment.

The preview uses no paid libraries, tracking pixels, autoplay, mailing-list service or collection form. Its optional YouTube player loads only after a click. Direct watch links remain available without the player. No new analytics or data collection is authorized by this source addition.

## Metadata and localization

Use the title and description drafts in `articles/` and the eight localized title/description sets in `localized-metadata.json`. Preserve the song names, artist name and production credits. Supplemental descriptions are not lyrics, exact quotes, transcripts or timed subtitles. Do not overwrite existing video metadata without reviewing the current version. Do not clone Sean's voice or replace his Jamaican identity.

## Measurement

Use existing authorized analytics or supplied official exports for website referrals, video watch time, returning viewers and subscriber changes. A source-tagged URL does not install analytics, and a click does not prove a subscription. Keep sent, replied, accepted, published, confirmed-airplay and confirmed-playlist statuses separate. Operational records remain outside this public repository.

## Cost and publication boundaries

Use zero-cost tools and channels only. Do not spend service credits or add paid services. Repository access does not confer YouTube editing access. Adding this directory makes no website or YouTube changes live and establishes no audience-growth baseline.
